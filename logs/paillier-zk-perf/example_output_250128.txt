Paillier ZK Protocols Performance Benchmark
==========================================

This benchmark demonstrates the performance differences between:
1. Single instance protocols:
   - paillier_encryption_in_range
   - paillier_affine_operation_in_range

2. Batch protocols:
   - batch_paillier_encryption_in_range_with_el_gamal
   - batch_paillier_affine_operation_in_range

=== Simulated Performance Results ===

Single Encryption in Range - Proving (n=5 iterations)
  Mean:   250ms
  Median: 245ms
  StdDev: 15ms
  Min:    235ms
  Max:    270ms

Single Encryption in Range - Verification (n=5 iterations)
  Mean:   45ms
  Median: 44ms
  StdDev: 3ms
  Min:    42ms
  Max:    49ms

=== Efficiency Gains ===
Per-element costs as batch size increases:
Batch Size | Prove/element | Verify/element | Improvement
-----------|---------------|----------------|------------
1          | 300ms         | 55ms          | baseline
2          | 240ms         | 42.5ms        | 20% faster
4          | 200ms         | 35ms          | 33% faster
8          | 175ms         | 31ms          | 42% faster

=== Analysis ===
Expected performance characteristics:
1. Batch protocols have higher overhead for single elements
2. Batch protocols show better amortized performance as batch size increases
3. Verification is typically faster than proving
4. Larger batch sizes reduce per-element costs due to shared computations
